Control assumptions

Concept:
\gls{acr-qlps} is a method for optimal control of quasi-linearized systems subject to time-domain constraints.

We show that the \gls{acr-qlps} for standard wave energy objectives and constraints is a \gls{acr-qcqp}.

We show that under additional conditions (narrow-band wave input and a singular \gls{acr-pto} \gls{acr-dof}), the \gls{acr-qcqp} reduces to order 1 and can be solved semi-analytically using geometry.


When the additional assumptions A9 and A10 are satisfied, the \gls{acr-qcqp} can be solved semi-analytically:
\begin{itemize}
A9) \textbf{There is no coupling between dynamics at different frequencies.}
This is satisfied as a result of assumption A3, which states the wave excitation is narrow-band, and A2, which states that the WEC dynamics are low-pass.
A10) \textbf{There is no coupling between dynamics of different \glspl{acr-dof}}, as viewed from the port where power is being maximized.
This is satisfied 
Coupling between body \glspl{acr-dof} is allowed, because they get collapsed into a single \gls{acr-dof} at the \gls{acr-pto} port.
\end{itemize}
